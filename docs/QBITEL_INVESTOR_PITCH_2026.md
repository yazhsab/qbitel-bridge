## 1. THE PROBLEM — THREE CONVERGING CRISES

### Crisis 1: The Legacy System Blind Spot
- **60% of Fortune 500** companies run critical operations on systems built 20–40 years ago — COBOL mainframes, SCADA controllers, proprietary industrial protocols
- These systems process **$3 trillion per day** in banking transactions, control power grids, manage patient records
- **Zero security vendors protect them** — CrowdStrike requires a modern OS agent, Palo Alto requires known protocols, Fortinet requires modern network stacks
- Modernisation costs $50M–$100M per system and **fails 60% of the time**

### Crisis 2: The Quantum Countdown
- Quantum computers will break RSA-2048 and ECDSA-256 — the encryption backbone of global finance, healthcare, and telecoms — by **2030–2033**
- Nation-states (confirmed by NSA, GCHQ, ENISA) are **harvesting encrypted traffic today** to decrypt it retroactively — "harvest now, decrypt later"
- SWIFT messages, wire transfers, patient records, and grid commands archived today are **already compromised for the future**
- NIST PQC standards are finalised; compliance mandates arrive 2025–2027. **No major security vendor offers a quantum-safe product today**
- Projected financial exposure from quantum decryption of banking archives: **$6.2 billion by 2030** (NIST/McKinsey)

### Crisis 3: The Speed Gap
- Average SOC response time: **65–140 minutes**
- In 65 minutes, attackers can exfiltrate 100GB of data, encrypt an entire network, or manipulate industrial controls
- Average cost: **$10–$50 per security event** with 67% analyst turnover from burnout
- SOAR tools follow static playbooks — they fail entirely against novel attack patterns on legacy systems they do not understand
