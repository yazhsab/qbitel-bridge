## 1. THE INDIA-SPECIFIC PROBLEM

### Why India Has the Most Urgent Need in Asia

**Banking:**
- RBI-regulated banks collectively process **₹400 Crore+ per second** across NEFT/RTGS/UPI
- 95% of PSB (Public Sector Bank) core banking runs on IBM mainframes (COBOL) — SBI's mainframe estate alone spans 4 decades
- RBI's **Master Direction on IT (2024)** mandates quantum-readiness assessments — banks have no compliant vendor available today
- SWIFT India traffic (₹8 lakh Crore/month in international remittances) is entirely RSA-encrypted — already vulnerable to harvest-now-decrypt-later

**Healthcare:**
- **ABDM (Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission)** mandates digital health records — but zero quantum-safe encryption exists on medical devices
- Government hospitals run on legacy systems with no security layer
- PHI (Protected Health Information) on Indian black markets: ₹3,000–₹8,000 per record

**Critical Infrastructure:**
- CERC (Central Electricity Regulatory Commission) and CERT-In have issued advisories — India's power grid SCADA systems are unprotected
- The 2020 Mumbai grid attack (attributed to Chinese state actors) demonstrated live vulnerability
- Water treatment, railways, and defence PSUs all use legacy protocols with zero PQC protection

**Government / Defence:**
- MeitY's **IndEA framework** and CERT-In empanelment requirements are creating mandatory security spend
- DRDO, ISRO, and defence PSUs need air-gapped AI — cloud-dependent foreign tools are disqualified
